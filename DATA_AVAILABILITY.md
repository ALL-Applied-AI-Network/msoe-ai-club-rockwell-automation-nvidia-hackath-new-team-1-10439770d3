# Data availability

The public repository contains source code, schemas, printable models, compact reports, selected plans, metrics, and consented representative images.

The following are not public:

- raw facility and phone videos;
- voice recordings;
- browser profiles and authentication state;
- physical camera calibration, installation-specific endpoints, and facility request credentials;
- raw robot exports containing detailed operational telemetry;
- generated frame caches and duplicate renders;
- third-party model checkpoints and cloned upstream repositories; and
- multi-gigabyte reconstruction intermediates.

Aggregate physical data counts and the evidence boundary are documented in [`docs/data-and-evidence.md`](docs/data-and-evidence.md). The final public demonstration is available at <https://youtu.be/5eQ3-p7v9-A>.

Compact commissioning reports retain a small number of historical TCP targets as experimental evidence. They are explicitly nonportable and are not accompanied by the facility client or any live robot transport.

Researchers seeking additional data should open an issue describing the requested artifact, intended use, storage plan, and privacy safeguards. Availability depends on participant consent, facility approval, event terms, and third-party rights.
